👋 Hi, I’m @mrbkarimi. I am deeply interested in the application of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data-driven methods to seismology, earthquake engineering, and structural risk assessment.

My background in structural engineering, structural dynamics, seismic response analysis, and vibration-based monitoring has shaped my interest in developing tools that can extract meaningful information from complex ground-motion and structural-response data. I am particularly motivated by research areas aligned with NORSAR’s work, including seismic monitoring, earthquake hazard assessment, signal processing, data interpretation, and risk-informed decision-making.

I am passionate about using AI and machine learning to accelerate time-consuming analytical tasks, identify hidden patterns in large datasets, improve predictive models, and support more reliable assessment of seismic and structural performance. In this context, I see strong potential for combining physics-based understanding with explainable and reproducible machine-learning approaches.

My goal is to contribute to research that improves our understanding of seismic events, supports safer infrastructure, and advances practical tools for earthquake risk reduction and resilient built environments.
